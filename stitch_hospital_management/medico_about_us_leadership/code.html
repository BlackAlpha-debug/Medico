OUR ORIGIN STORY

Born from a Commitment to Accessible, World-Class Medicine

In 2008, Dr. Evelyn Sterling and Dr. David Chen saw a persistent disconnect between rapid pharmacological advancements and the human experience inside modern hospitals. They set out to build an institution where diagnostic precision and compassionate doctor-patient bonds weren't mutually exclusive.

Starting as a specialized ambulatory center with just twelve physicians, Medico steadily broke new ground in regional surgical outcomes, preventative cardiology, and transparent patient advocacy. Over the past fifteen years, that initial clinic has flourished into a multi-campus, tertiary academic healthcare ecosystem spanning 450+ patient suites, state-level trauma readiness, and an interdisciplinary medical research institute.
